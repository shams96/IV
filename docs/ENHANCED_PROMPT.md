# Enhanced Build Prompt — Isola Vitale

Create a light, editorial luxury-brand website for **Isola Vitale**, a private Mediterranean island wellness house. The identity should feel quiet, expensive, human, and contemporary rather than dark or nightclub-like.

## Brand direction

- **Name:** Isola Vitale
- **Emblem:** a luxury **IV** monogram that works as a crest, navigation seal, and campaign mark.
- **Palette:** ivory, porcelain, champagne gold, warm stone, olive leaf, and restrained terracotta accents.
- **Tone:** private, sensory, refined, restorative, by-invitation.
- **Avoid:** dark-theme dominance, visual clutter, generic spa clichés, and heavy media that slows the site.

## 2026 web direction applied

- Hero-led expressive typography with campaign-scale editorial rhythm.
- Bento-style modular content cards for scannable luxury storytelling.
- Soft organic gradients, tactile grain, and glass-like light surfaces.
- Subtle micro-interactions and scroll reveals with reduced-motion support.
- Adaptive concierge selector to preview personalized journeys without invasive data collection.
- Performance-first implementation using CSS-native visuals instead of large image/video assets.
- Accessibility-minded structure, contrast, semantic landmarks, and keyboard-friendly controls.

## Required sections

1. Sticky light navigation with IV monogram and invitation CTA.
2. Hero section introducing the private island wellness house.
3. House-code narrative explaining quiet luxury positioning.
4. Signature experience bento grid.
5. Adaptive journey selector for Renewal, Celebration, and Residence stays.
6. Ritual calendar describing the day-by-day sensory wellness arc.
7. Journal cards explaining design, technology, and access principles.
8. Footer with brand mark and 2026 copyright.
